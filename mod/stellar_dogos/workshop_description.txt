Stellar Xeno

Custom Stellaris species portraits from your own images.

Stellar Xeno adds selectable static species portraits for Stellaris 4.4.x (Pegasus). Subscribe, enable the mod in the launcher, then pick a portrait when creating a species of the matching type.

Included portraits (examples): Mammalian (Piglet, Oakley, Angus, and more), Avian, Reptilian, and Fungoid registrations shipped in this package.

This Workshop item is the game mod only. It does not include the local Portrait Creator tools used to generate and register new portraits on a developer machine.

Compatibility: Stellaris v4.4.*
